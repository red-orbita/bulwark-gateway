Response Cache

Exact-match caching of LLM responses (tenant-isolated SHA-256 keys) to cut cost and latency. The runtime kill-switch below writes to Redis and the proxy honors it within ~5s without a restart. Disabling the cache guarantees fresh policy/IOC evaluation on every request. All mutations are audit-logged.
