Adaptive Correlation Engine

Correlates security signals per origin into a decaying risk score. Risk accrues from confirmed input↔output exfiltration and from ongoing WARN/BLOCK events folded in by the event tap. When an origin crosses the threshold, the next request from that origin is hardened — WARN, or BLOCK when blocking is on. Config changes below write to Redis and the proxy honors them within ~5s without a restart. Origin keys are irreversible SHA-256 digests. All mutations are audit-logged. This is inline enforcement only — multi-source, forensic, and cross-tenant correlation is delegated to your SIEM.

Enforcement Tuning

Lower thresholds = more aggressive hardening (higher recall, more false positives). Decay is the risk half-life in seconds. Leave a field blank to keep its current effective value; only changed fields are written. The correlation_enabled master switch is a boot-time setting and is not tunable here.

Blocking enforcement

On: origins over the BLOCK threshold get 403. Off: hardening is WARN-only (logged, forwarded).
