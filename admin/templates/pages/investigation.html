Investigation Center

A SOC triage workspace over the correlation engine's alerts. Pick a correlated block/warn alert to see the full evidence chain — the confirmed input↔output exfiltration incident joined to the exact detections that produced it, the per-signal confidence breakdown, and an at-risk origin's decayed risk alongside the durable ledger of events that drove it up. Acknowledge, assign, resolve, and attach investigation notes. Evidence is reconstructed entirely from the durable event store and existing risk state — this view adds no request-path cost.
