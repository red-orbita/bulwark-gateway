# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Bulwark Gateway welcomes contributors and users regardless of age, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education, nationality,
appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation. Participation
should be respectful, constructive and free from harassment.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat people with empathy and respect, including when disagreeing.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not personal attacks.
- Accept constructive criticism and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy, consent and other people's time.
- Help newcomers and keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, personal attacks or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and
other project-managed spaces. It also applies when officially representing the
project in public. Maintainers are subject to the same expectations.

## Reporting

To request contact about a conduct concern, open an
[issue](https://github.com/red-orbita/bulwark-gateway/issues/new)
titled "Conduct contact request". Include only a request to speak with a
maintainer. **The issue is public: do not include names of affected people,
incident details, screenshots, contact details or other sensitive information.**
Wait for a maintainer to arrange an appropriate follow-up channel before sharing
those details. The project does not currently publish a direct private conduct
reporting address; this public contact-request process cannot guarantee privacy
or an immediate response. Do not use security advisories for conduct reports.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, ask for corrective action,
issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently exclude a
participant depending on severity and repetition. Immediate restrictions may be
appropriate for threats or serious harassment. Decisions should be proportionate
and communicated with reasons when feasible, without exposing private details.
Maintainers involved in a report should defer its review to another uninvolved
maintainer where available. A participant may request reconsideration through
the same contact-request process, without reposting sensitive details publicly.
